# Terminal Is Your Friend instructor

The AI instructor's provider protocol, teaching style and bounded notebook/chat
design are adapted from [Pengeszikra/terminal-is-your-friend](https://github.com/Pengeszikra/terminal-is-your-friend),
notably `server/instructor.mjs`, `server/instructor-context.mjs` and
`src/instructor-memory.ts` (source snapshot tree `dcf05836d5646b365b2088e0aff5afeca1461919`).
HEXAW supplies its own WASM/assembly curriculum, shell state and command UI.
